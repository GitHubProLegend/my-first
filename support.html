Support

Everything you might need to ask.

And a form at the bottom that reaches a person, not a queue.

Shipping

Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day. Standard delivery is two to five business days within the continental US; express is next business day. Hoops ship freight and take seven to ten days, with a scheduled delivery window.

Free over $75, $6 flat below that. Express is $18. Freight on in-ground hoops is included in the listed price.

Returns

Yes. That is the point. Sixty days from delivery, used condition accepted, return shipping covered. Gear you were afraid to use was never actually tested.

Back to the original payment method within three business days of the return being scanned in. You do not have to wait for it to reach our warehouse.

Warranty

Two years on balls and bags, one year on electronics, five years on hoop structures. Covers manufacturing and material failure — seam separation, delamination, bladder leaks, structural welds. Normal wear from play is not a defect, though if a cover wears out unusually fast we want to see it.

Send photos and your order number through the form below. Most claims are resolved the same day, and we ship the replacement before the old one comes back.

Sizing

29.5" for men’s and boys’ play from age 15, 28.5" for women’s play and ages 9–14, and 27.5" for younger players. If a player is between sizes, the smaller one protects their shooting form.

True to length, slightly narrow through the midfoot because of the containment cage. Wide feet should go up a half size. Sixty-day returns apply to footwear too, so trying it on the court is the reliable test.
