Our standard

Equipment should be the last thing you think about.

The best gear disappears. You notice a ball when the grip goes, a shoe when it slides, a rim when it eats a shot that should have gone. Everything we make is aimed at the moment you stop noticing.

How we test

Five hundred hours before it gets a page.

Nothing ships on a spec sheet alone.

Wear

Covers run against calibrated abrasion drums until pebble height drops below spec. We publish the hour count, not a rating.

Pressure

Every bladder design sits inflated for ninety days. Anything drifting past 0.2 PSI goes back to the drawing board.

People

Forty players across six programs get pre-production units for a full season, and their complaints outrank our lab data.

What we won’t do

A short line, on purpose.

Eight products. We would rather have one ball that is the right answer than six that hedge. When something better exists, the old one leaves the line — it does not get a discount tier and a second life.

No colourway churn. No model year on the box. If we change something, it is because the change is better, and we will tell you exactly what it was.
