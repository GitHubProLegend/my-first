Why this one

Three things a ball has to get right.

Everything else is marketing.

Grip

The texture goes all the way through.

A printed cover is a coating, and coatings wear off — usually somewhere around the middle of a season, right when you have stopped thinking about the ball. Ours is pressed into full-grain composite, so what you feel in month eight is what you felt on day one.

Bounce

True off every floor.

A butyl bladder wound with nylon thread holds pressure inside 0.2 PSI across a season. What comes back off the boards is exactly what you put into it.

Channels

3.2 mm deep.

Deep enough to find with your fingertips without looking, shallow enough that the ball still rolls true off the rim.

Weight

22 oz, checked twice.

Every ball is weighed at the factory and again at our warehouse. Anything outside half an ounce does not ship.

Break-in

None required.

It ships at game feel. There is no surface layer that has to wear off before the grip arrives.
